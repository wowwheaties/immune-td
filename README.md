# Immune System Tower Defense

Defend a human stomach from an invading infection.

Pathogens march along the gastric tract; you spend **ATP** to place immune cells that
kill them before they reach the far end. Every leak costs the organ — and you can see it
happening, because the stomach itself sickens as you lose.

## How to play

- **Place a defender** — tap a cell in the bottom bar, then tap open tissue. On a phone
  it's a two-step confirm tap; with a mouse it's a single click.
- **Start Wave** begins the next assault. Kills earn ATP, so trading early defence for
  income is a real decision.
- **Sell** a placed defender by selecting it and using the Sell button.
- The tissue is not uniform: **pale patches** make a defender fire ~25% faster, and
  **inflamed tissue** slows it by ~50%. Placement matters.

## Your defenders

| Cell | Role |
|---|---|
| Neutrophil | Cheap, rapid fire — the backbone |
| Macrophage | Engulfs; area damage and slow |
| Antibody | Long range, marks targets |
| T-Cell | Short range, heavy single-target damage |
| Natural Killer | Long range, hunts the tough ones |
| Cytokine | Buffs neighbouring defenders |
| Fever | Area damage over time, burns everything nearby |

## Pathogens

Bacteria swarm, viruses are fast, fungus is tanky, parasites are slow and strong — and
each mission ends with a boss that will punch through a lazy build.
